Purple01 is an early P4A concept page centred on joyful responsible abundance,
the "Purple Synthesis", democratic surges and practical stewardship.

## Public page

- [Open Purple01](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/purple01/)

## What is here

`index.html` contains the complete single-page presentation. It introduces:

- the proposed rhythm of major and minor democratic surges;
- a decentralised foundation model;
- braided-economy, ethical-overcompliance and resilience ideas; and
- interactive P4A concept cards.

## Open locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or backend is required.

## Status and boundaries

This is an exploratory political design artefact, not a registered party,
approved platform, legal structure or promise of implementation. Policy,
electoral, financial and organisational claims need current primary-source
checking and democratic review before use.
